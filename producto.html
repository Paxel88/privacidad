MKM Stock Exporter

A Chrome extension for trading-card buyers: scan a Cardmarket seller's stock, export it to CSV, value the lot and prepare your purchase offer.

Get the extension Subscribe to Premium

What it does

MKM Stock Exporter reads the card listings that are publicly displayed on a Cardmarket seller's offers page (that the user opens) and exports them to a CSV file. It also values the lot and helps the buyer prepare a fair purchase offer — all inside the user's own browser. It is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with Cardmarket.

Features

Free

  • Full seller-stock scan (no duplicates)
  • "Sellable card" filters (price, condition, foil, language)
  • Lot valuation
  • Summary CSV export (for Excel)

Premium (subscription)

  • Export to the TCG Power Tools format
  • Offer calculator + seller message generator
  • Lot analytics (value concentration, price tiers, splits)

How the subscription works (fulfillment)

  1. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Click the cart button in the extension, or the "Subscribe" button above, to open the Lemon Squeezy checkout.
  3. After subscribing, you receive a license key on the confirmation page and by email.
  4. Paste the key into the extension's "License key" field and click Activate.
  5. The key is validated against our licensing endpoint and the Premium features unlock instantly. Everything is digital and automatic — no physical goods.

Delivery method: 100% digital. The product is a browser extension distributed via the Chrome Web Store; Premium is unlocked with a license key delivered by Lemon Squeezy after purchase.

Privacy

Everything is processed in the user's browser. No card data or personal data is sent to any server. The only data transmitted is the license key, used solely to validate the subscription. See our Privacy Policy.
